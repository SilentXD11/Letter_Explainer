📬 Letter Explainer

Take a photo of a confusing letter (or upload a PDF). We'll explain it in simple words, list what you need to do and by when, and draft a reply.

Tips for a readable photo
  • Lay the letter flat on a dark table, in daylight.
  • Turn the flash off to avoid glare.
  • Hold the phone straight above the page, with all four corners visible.
  • Tap the screen on the text to focus, then hold still.
